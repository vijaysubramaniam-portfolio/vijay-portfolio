My perspective

Why human writing still matters in the age of AI

AI can generate words at lightning speed. But there are things only a human writer can truly deliver — and that gap matters more than ever.

Empathy cannot be automated

Great content speaks to real people with real problems. A human writer understands nuance, emotion, and cultural context in ways AI still struggles with. When your reader feels genuinely understood, they trust your brand — and trust drives conversions.

Original thought beats pattern matching

AI generates content by predicting what comes next based on existing data. It cannot form a genuinely new perspective, draw from lived experience, or make a bold claim that challenges the status quo. Original human thought is what makes content worth reading — and worth linking to.

Google rewards experience and expertise

With E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) at the heart of Google's quality guidelines, content written by someone with real-world experience consistently outranks generic AI output. Search engines are getting better at telling the difference.

Brand voice is a human fingerprint

Your brand's tone, personality, and storytelling style are what make you memorable. AI can approximate a voice — but a skilled human writer crafts it, protects it, and evolves it with intention. That's the difference between content that's forgettable and content that builds a loyal audience.

Strategy requires judgment, not just speed

Knowing what to write, when to write it, and who to write it for is a strategic skill built through years of experience. AI is a powerful tool — but it needs a human strategist to direct it toward goals that actually move the needle for your business.
